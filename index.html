How to get the email file
  • Gmail: open the sent email, tap ⋮ (More) and choose Download message.
  • Outlook on the web: open the email, choose … then Save or Download.
  • Outlook for Mac: drag the email from the list onto your desktop.
  • Outlook for Windows: choose Start blank and paste, then copy the email body in.
  • Yahoo Mail: choose … then View raw message, copy it all and paste it into the body box.
